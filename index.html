Welcome to Elisa Blake

Elisa Blake is an AI-powered chat companion app for iOS. Chat with Elisa, a friendly AI character who remembers your conversations and adapts to your personality — great for practicing conversation, unwinding after a long day, or just having someone to talk to.

Features

  • Natural AI Conversations: Chat with Elisa, an AI character that adapts to your tone and interests.
  • Conversation Memory: Elisa remembers details you share so conversations feel continuous and personal.
  • Conversation Modes: Switch between different conversation styles — thoughtful, playful, calm, and more.
  • Safe by Design: Built-in safety filters keep conversations respectful and appropriate for a general audience.
  • Privacy First: Clear disclosure and consent before any data is shared with our AI provider. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Important Notice

Elisa is an artificial intelligence character, not a real person. The App is intended for entertainment purposes and is not a substitute for professional advice, therapy, or counseling. Users must be at least 17 years old.
